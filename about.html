About Zayka Harvest

Your most trusted source for fresh fruits in Karachi, committed to delivering 100% organic and farm-fresh produce.

Our Story & Mission

Founded in Karachi, Zayka Harvest was established with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between local orchards and your dining table. We believe that every family deserves access to chemical-free, nutrient-rich fruits without hassle.

Why Choose Our Organic Store?

🍓 Direct from Farms: We handpick our items directly from trusted farmers in Sindh and Punjab.

🚚 Same-Day Delivery: Fast and reliable home delivery across DHA, Clifton, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, and PECHS.

✨ Quality Guaranteed: Strict quality checks ensure only the freshest fruits reach your home.
